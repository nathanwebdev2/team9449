[Honeycomb was our 2026 REBUILT competition robot, designed around a fast, consistent scoring cycle and the flexibility to adapt throughout the season. Built on a REV MaxSwerve drivetrain, the 95 lb robot combined a slapdown intake, vector wheel kicker, angled passive hopper floor, and a 12.5-inch-wide drum shooter into a compact scoring system. A stretchy net extended the hopper capacity, allowing us to carry significantly more game pieces without adding a large mechanical system.

We are especially proud of how effectively the mechanisms worked together. The passive hopper reduced complexity while maintaining reliable feeding, and the wide drum shooter gave us the ability to shoot 10 fuel in a second out of this robot with accuracy grater than 95%. The combination of high-speed swerve and a robust intake also allowed Honeycomb to move quickly between cycles and stay effective in traffic.

If we built Honeycomb again, we would focus on improving mechanism serviceability, increasing overall weight and rigidity, and making more components easier to access and replace during competition. The robot taught us a lot about balancing speed, reliability, packaging, and iteration under the pressure of a full competition season.
